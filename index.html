Questions

Before you call.

How much will my project cost?

It depends on the size of the area, the materials, and the site itself, so we price each project after the site visit. You'll have a written proposal before any work starts.

What areas do you serve?

All of St. Mary's County, Maryland, including the towns listed above. If you're just outside the county, ask us.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We're licensed by the Maryland Home Improvement Commission (MHIC #[license number]) and insured.

How quickly will I hear back?

Within one business day. We'll set a time to walk the property with you.

What should I have ready for the site visit?

A rough budget range, photos of landscapes you like, and a property survey or plat if you have one. None of it is required.
